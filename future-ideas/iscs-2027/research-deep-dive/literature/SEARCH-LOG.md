## Coverage and access limits

Primary full/HTML methods were inspected for the most threatening trajectory and segmentation papers. Some sources were only abstract-level; depth is recorded in the topic map. OpenReview challenge pages and a ScienceDirect 403 prevented direct access on some routes; arXiv author copies or official proceedings were used where available. A failed OpenReview fetch is not evidence that the paper is unavailable or unreviewed.

Results with clinical applications were considered only as methodological comparators. No clinical deployment, diagnosis, new human data collection, or transfer of clinical validity is proposed. Geographic “semantic trajectories” literature was screened out when it concerned mobility rather than language.

The independent [cross-sectional search](cross-sectional.md) and [network/memory search](networks-memory.md) record their own primary sources and queries. Searches will need refreshing before a final novelty claim or submission; recent preprints can change the comparison substantially.
