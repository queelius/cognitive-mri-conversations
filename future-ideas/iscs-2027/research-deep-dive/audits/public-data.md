# Public-data feasibility for cross-sectional CMRI

Audit date: 2026-10-15. **Choose public conversation-level construct validation before claiming comparisons among people's knowledge archives.** Large chat datasets contain many rows, but participant linkage and usable history length are the binding constraints. A small completed check is reported in [public-cross-section/REPORT.md](../checks/public-cross-section/REPORT.md); the frozen protocol, hashes, code, tests and results are beside it.

## What the bounded sample establishes

The measured slice has 37,208 rows, 36,821 unique content hashes and 9,645 IP proxies. Median history size is one; 77.3% of proxies occur on one UTC date. With at least 50 unique conversations and five dates, 51 proxies qualify before screening, 30 afterward. At 100 conversations, only four remain after screening. These are **within-shard lower-coverage counts**, not full histories, validated persons, or population estimates. The preselected first 2,000 content rows form 787 IP/template components; 406 contain an eligible representative for the role check. Components include all acquired rows before language or other eligibility filters, retaining bridges carried by excluded records. The earlier 420-component count came from an incorrect filtered grouping and is superseded. [Measured evidence and correction](../checks/public-cross-section/REPORT.md)

Three distinctions are essential:

1. **Conversation hash is content, not identity.** Identical text can recur under different timestamps/proxies; the card explicitly says the hash is not unique. The check uses the full eight-field source key to join labels and rejects conflicting duplicate keys. Unique turn identifiers are available in source messages for later event-level deduplication.
2. **IP linkage is a proxy.** Shared networks and changing addresses cause opposite errors; geography/headers are diagnostics rather than proof of a person. Activity filtering can preferentially remove precisely the large histories sought. Content/template components must be built before filtering and stay on one split. Unacquired source records can still carry unknown bridges. Account-level claims need independently established linkage.
3. **Selection is part of the estimand.** Free API-backed chat access, language detection, content filtering, changing models, automated use and large prompt templates all alter the observed distribution. Topic, platform and country contrasts remain descriptive. A pre-September-2024 cutoff avoids the later spike identified by Adopt != Adapt but does not eliminate earlier templates.

## Human validation option and leakage hazards

ThoughtTrace's collection is particularly useful because users privately supplied reasons/reactions unavailable to the assistant. But its participants completed two self-defined tasks, each within ten minutes. Post-task summaries and expectations are retrospective; reason/reaction **category labels were assigned by an LLM**, whereas their underlying texts are user reports. The original paper already compares message–thought embeddings and conversation stages. Neither the task setting nor self-report establishes a comprehensive cognitive ground truth. [Methods and annotation details](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20087v1)

The released reason taxonomy includes `task_motivation`, `task_continuation`, `task_reorientation`, `content_expectation`, `style_expectation`, `context_grounding_and_constraints`, and `social_and_others`. Reaction types are `explicit_affirmation`, `partial_satisfaction`, `presentation_style`, `scope_fit`, and `content_relevance`. Appendix D.6 specifies asymmetric annotation context: GPT-5.4 assigns reason types using user history and the reported reason while omitting full assistant responses; reaction typing uses the assistant response and reported reaction. Category-based role comparisons can therefore inherit the annotation design. [Primary annotation specification](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20087v1)

For a new study, quarantine all thought text, labels, post-hoc summaries/expectations and demographic answers from graph construction and predictor inputs. Keep every conversation/task from one participant in one fold, and connect repeated templates across participants before splitting. Use only dialogue available at the claimed prediction point; subsequent turns can reveal reactions and retrospectively complete the task. Freeze topic/stage/length matching using development data. Category prediction can be secondary, explicitly measuring agreement with an LLM taxonomy; a primary test should use the independent reported text. Original participants' statements and third-party judgments in Re-Centering Humans are different forms of evidence and should not be pooled as equivalent truth.

**Strongest proposed falsifiable question:** Does role-separated graph organization of a dialogue prefix improve recovery of that user's reported purpose or constraint over equally budgeted direct-text and topic/stage baselines, and does inclusion of assistant text create spurious apparent purpose connections? This is a measurement question about representations, not a causal claim that assistant use changed cognition.

An affordable follow-up can rank the actual reported reason against same-topic, same-stage, length-matched reasons from other held-out participants. Compare a strong user-text embedding model, a dialogue embedding, a lexical model, topic/stage/length/model controls, and the same representations plus predeclared graph features. Match predictor capacity and tune only within development participant folds. Score paired retrieval accuracy and calibration, then aggregate/bootstrapping by participant. Use a held-out-topic analysis and assistant-message swaps within topic/model/length strata as negative controls; swaps test representation contamination, not human behavior. Exclude or stratify verbatim prompt–reason overlap before outcome inspection. A practical go criterion could be ≥5 pp extra held-out accuracy with a participant-bootstrap interval above zero, sustained across two encoders and absent for matched-shuffled graph features. Failing that criterion should reject graph-specific added value, even if ordinary embeddings recover purpose. No such experiment has yet been run.

## Acquisition decision

For a March submission, public data access is **not** the chief obstacle for a narrow measurement paper. Novelty and construct validation are. Do not spend effort relabelling millions of WildChat conversations or building a people-level comparison from IP groups before a small independent-target test passes. A full metadata scan could count globally eligible WildChat proxies cheaply via projected columns, but it cannot repair identity validity. The raw-data access rules for donated-history studies should be treated separately: [Bowling with ChatGPT](https://github.com/saikeerthana00/Bowling_with_ChatGPT) releases code/prompts, while the [four-country history study](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38279v1) explicitly withholds full histories and offers only defined academic access under a data-use agreement. Neither was available as unrestricted archival data in this audit.
