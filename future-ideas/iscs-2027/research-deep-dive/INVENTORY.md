## Reconciled contradictions

1. **Prefix experiment status:** saved Exp04 scores, the addendum and manuscript agree it ran; stale NOT RUN/no-manuscript notes are superseded in this audit. No paid rerun is needed to resolve status.
2. **Hierarchy identity:** child CLAUDE.md still describes agentic delegation; the actual conference manuscript is the concept hierarchy. Both histories exist, but they are not the same study.
3. **Chronology:** only three embedding batch timestamps exist in the current DB. Sorting by them is not event ordering; insertion order needs its own validation. The hierarchy's headline order is alphabetical. A separate chronological growth output prevents claiming the effect necessarily vanishes.
4. **Corpus availability/counts:** 1,888 files are physically present. The stored statistics and manuscript give nine exclusions; older prose says two. Neither explains the entire difference from the 1,908 raw-archive count (1,908−9 is 1,899). The stats generator contains declared exclusion counts; it is not a lineage reconciliation. A source-to-release inclusion manifest would resolve this. [Aggregate record](results/corpus-inventory.json)
5. **Models and dimensions:** older outlines/specifications and current observed embeddings disagree. Current DB evidence supports one observed small/256d model; a claimed cross-model alpha replication remains unverified, rather than disproved.
6. **Inference labels:** high modularity is not validated intent; different partitions are not independence; fitted decay is an objective-specific filter parameter; projection clustering is not evidence of a shared cognitive mechanism; “recency” and “porosity” must match their implemented definitions.

Original notes and sources were left intact. This linked inventory supersedes them for the research decision without erasing history.

## Evidence ledger to preserve

**Surviving positives:** useful static organization/visualization; genuine conditional order effects in some motion statistics; ordinary smoothing improves next-vector cosine over running mean; a positive prefix-approximation point estimate remains after leave-one-conversation-out selection, although its selection-aware interval spans zero; existing public-data plumbing is inexpensive.

**Failed predictions/counterexamples:** the original two-part dynamics gate failed; motifs did not beat their null; exact prefix-position retrieval missed its target; fitted alpha did not beat fixed .85 in the new bounded sample; the universal −1/2 theorem and shape comparator fail explicit examples; temporal lifecycle and recency measurements fail elementary fixtures; the hierarchy null interpretation changes; bridge retention lacks a validated mechanism or stable incremental value. These are research results to retain, not tuning targets to erase.

**Not yet established:** public generalization of the dynamics findings; an intrinsic semantic timescale; knowledge/learning/transfer inferred from graphs; causal platform effects; a natural hierarchy; retrieval utility from bridges; reliable person-level long archives; a novel full-paper contribution. The [candidate briefs](BRIEFS.md) specify what would change those judgments.
