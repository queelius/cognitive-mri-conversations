A calibration paper may be justified by a reproducible, consequential failure even without a better method, but a list of algebraic identities or isolated bugs is insufficient.

Keep this to a decision pilot, approximately 1–2 focused researcher-weeks in scope. Finish with reproducible public results, uncertainty, figures, remaining limitations and a pursue/narrow/abandon decision.
