# ISCS 2027: verified planning constraints

Checked 2026-10-15 against the [official home page](https://iutdijon.u-bourgogne.fr/ccs-france/) and [submission page](https://iutdijon.u-bourgogne.fr/ccs-france/submissions/).

- Porto, Portugal, June 2–4, 2027.
- Submission deadline March 1, 2027; acceptance notification March 30; author registration April 19.
- Long papers: 12 pages, unpublished work. Short papers: 8 pages, unpublished work. Extended abstracts: 2–4 pages, published or unpublished research.
- English, LaTeX template, CMT submission; at least two reviewers.
- Relevant scope includes complex networks, computational social science, AI/simulation, cognition and linguistics; societal impact is emphasized.

**October 16 UTC / October 15 local update:** the directly inspected [official publication page](https://iutdijon.u-bourgogne.fr/ccs-france/publication/) now supplies additional evidence: it names Springer *Studies in Systems, Decision and Control* proceedings and selected extended-paper / unpublished-research-abstract invitations to an *Applied Network Science* fast track. The page is labeled Porto/2027 but retains a 2026 book-of-abstracts paragraph, so this is mixed-edition official text, not a clean publication contract.

Copyright/licensing, preprint eligibility, simultaneous submission, minimum journal-extension increment and reuse permissions remain unresolved. A fast-track invitation does not settle those conditions. The original October 15 audit had inspected only home/submission pages and correctly left the publisher unknown; this additional source supersedes that coverage limitation. No organizer was contacted and no submission made. The [temporal literature/venue audit](../../../papers/temporal-networks-calibration/pilot/audits/LITERATURE-AND-VENUE.md) records sources and inspection limits.

The scientific fit is strongest for a focused complex-systems measurement question with a demonstrated consequence. A generic AI tool or visualization requires a separate scientific contribution. An existing published paper could support an extended abstract if a new full paper fails its evidence gate; that is a fallback format, not a new research result.
