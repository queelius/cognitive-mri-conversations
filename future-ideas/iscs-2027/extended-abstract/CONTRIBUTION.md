Working title: *When Observation Choices Resemble Dynamics in AI Conversation

fixed-graph revelation has exact growth expectations, and a quantified forecasting

## Argument outline

1. Earlier CMRI work represents conversational content as a graph or hierarchy.
   Those descriptive objects do not independently measure memory or learning.
2. Fixed endpoint edges are revealed as vertices arrive. Exact expected edge and
   active-node counts expose construction effects without an assumption of semantic
   clustering. They are established probability calculations, not novel theory.
3. In exposed PRISM results, the role interaction of broad-minus-recent history
   changes when the current user request is withheld from assistant prediction.
   A longer assistant forecast horizon is part of that changed estimand. Preserve
   the positive next-user history result and the remaining MiniLM role contrast.
4. Report observation units, feature availability, denominator, predictive prefix
   and what each control preserves before making developmental interpretations.

## What is and is not added

The abstract adds a connected presentation and a reproducible transfer of the
existing calibration evidence. It does not add a new cohort, independent
confirmation, estimator, generative mechanism, cognitive outcome or validation of
the physics drafts. Temporal reference models and context-dependent history value
are prior work. The paper does not claim that observed order effects all vanish.

The strongest empirical panel is a secondary, exposed development contrast.
The primary surviving user-history gain remains useful but does not establish
incremental older information beyond every content explanation. Neither the small
learned-readout negatives nor the AI-only revision inventory is used to strengthen
the conclusion.

This is a defensible conference-discussion contribution if the authors want a
measurement-focused presentation. It does not satisfy the earlier preference for
a new independently supported behavioral discovery. That distinction is why the
recommendation is an extended abstract, not an 8/12-page paper.
