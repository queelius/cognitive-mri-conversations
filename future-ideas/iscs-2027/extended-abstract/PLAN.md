## Purpose and design

Assess whether existing verified results support one coherent extended abstract
about the observation process in conversation-network and state measurements.
Compare a focused measurement argument with a published-work recap and a broad
portfolio synthesis. Prefer the focused argument only if its evidence and limits
are concrete. Do not call a collection of negative checks a new discovery.

The package lives in this new umbrella planning directory. Source paper
repositories stay read-only; existing umbrella edits are preserved. Local raw
conversations, model caches and all 248 reserve components are excluded. Only
public or already releasable aggregate evidence is used. No fitted model, new
natural outcome, recruitment, full paper or submission is authorized.
