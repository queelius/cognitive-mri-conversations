## Inspected evidence deliberately not promoted into the abstract

| Line | Preserved result and why it is not another abstract finding |
|---|---|
| Selective forgetting | No stable bridge-specific benefit; synthetic supports had zero participation at the primary graph setting and no clear benchmark cross-topic evaluation cases. This is a distinct construct problem, not evidence against all semantic networks. |
| Semantic measurement | Primary −4.89 pp with selection-aware [−8.58,+4.76] in a fixed retrieval bank; other visibility/encoder intervals do not exclude a five-point gain. Does not establish universal inferiority of sequence methods. |
| Temporal selected archives | Failed beta power/paper gate, undefined .95 fits, a preserved positive selected public residual and shorter-lag diagnostics. No population CI from selected archive proxies; omitted from the new headline rather than erased. |
| Useful-history learned controls | Frozen compressed readouts are limited; their negatives do not establish recent-context sufficiency. The synthesis uses pooling and origin contrasts only. |
| Revision/later-use pilot and event assessment | Zero clear same-premise revisions in AI drafts; no independent labels or persistence denominator. These do not establish natural absence or empirical validity of a new cognitive measure. |
| Hierarchy and physics drafts | Fixed cluster cuts are design choices; structural differences do not imply independence; high embedding agreement does not validate a physical force model. The earlier audits remain binding. |

## Forbidden upgrades

No inference of learning, intrinsic semantic half-life, causal assistant influence,
verified individuals, population prevalence, universal null order effects,
independence of two encoders, or a new full-paper contribution. No statistical
pooling across these heterogeneous pilots is attempted.
