## Track 2: consolidate and explore semantic dynamics

The user wants to retain this line alongside the conference priority and suggests merging the two physics-oriented drafts if their distinct ideas merit it. The source comparison supports **one planned manuscript lineage**, drawing on both repositories. A June 13 merged outline already proposed this, but its scientific claims need revision after the October audits. See [what overlaps, what to retain and what to test](DYNAMICS-CONSOLIDATION.md).

A possible unifying question is: **what state representation of a conversation is sufficient to describe its development and predict a useful observable?** Prefix approximation supplies a representation target; episodes, recurrence and role-specific development supply candidate applications. Physics-inspired models can remain hypotheses with explicit assumptions and discriminating predictions.

State reconstruction, externally validated episode/development analysis and stronger dynamical-model comparisons can all be investigated in the [portfolio](EXPERIMENT-PORTFOLIO.md). Choose the merged paper's main argument after comparing their evidence. These questions may also supply methods for the ISCS study. The conference paper should not depend on settling a broad theory of semantic physics.
