# Pooling diagnostics on development embeddings (editorial review, 2026-10-19)

Run by the reviewing session with `pooling_diagnostics.py` (same pooling, normalization,
component aggregation and 2,000-draw component bootstrap, seed 104729, as `cmri_wave`).
Inputs: `papers/portfolio-wave1/cache/encoded` (PRISM development: fit, tune and probe,
1,191 conversations, 360 components) and `papers/wildchat-replication/cache/development`
(WildChat development: 599 conversations, 345 components). The PRISM reserve and the
WildChat confirmation sample were not read. User targets at positions 4, 6, 8, 10.
Usage: `python -I pooling_diagnostics.py <cache dir> <out.json>` from the umbrella root
with the `papers/iscs-2027/.venv` interpreter.

Replication check: MEAN - WIN2 on the PRISM probe split gives +.0344 (MiniLM) and
+.0192 (E5), the pre-registered development values; on WildChat development it gives
+.0269 and +.0174, the values in the replication report. The replica is faithful.

Predictors (all mean-pooled then unit-normalized; raw cosine against the unit target,
centered cosine subtracts the user-role center estimated on the fit split from both):
MEAN = all prior messages; WIN2 / WIN4 = trailing windows; FIRST = the user's opening
message alone; OPEN2 = opening exchange; FIRST+WIN2 = opening message plus the last
two; PREV_USER+WIN2 = the user's message before the latest exchange plus the last two
(equals FIRST+WIN2 at position 4); OWN_MEAN = all prior user messages; RAND2 = one
random prior user message and one random prior assistant message (the within-conversation
shuffle null for the last two); DONOR_* = the same pool sizes filled with messages of
matching role drawn at random from conversations in OTHER components (one draw per case,
so per-position donor rows carry Monte Carlo noise of a few thousandths);
WIN2+DONOR_REST = the real last two messages plus unrelated donor messages in place of
the older history.

Findings in brief:
1. FIRST+WIN2 - WIN2 equals or exceeds MEAN - WIN2 in both collections and both
   encoders; MEAN - FIRST+WIN2 is zero or negative. At positions 6 to 10,
   PREV_USER+WIN2 does as well as FIRST+WIN2, so the opening is not special: the
   gain over the last exchange is the gain from a second user turn. MEAN - WIN4 is
   at most about .01 and on WildChat not distinguishable from zero under centering.
2. DONOR_MEAN - DONOR_WIN2 (pooling j versus 2 unrelated vectors) is +.020 to +.033
   in raw cosine, the size of the headline effect, and zero under centering. In E5,
   adding unrelated vectors to the real last-two window RAISES raw cosine
   (WIN2+DONOR_REST - WIN2 about +.01 in both collections). Raw cosine therefore
   cannot distinguish informative history from more vectors; centered cosine can.
   The synthetic null B (isotropic independent vectors) does not reproduce this
   because real encoders are anisotropic (single-message cosine to the user centroid
   is .22 for MiniLM and .87 for E5). On the held-out samples the fraction of
   components with a positive gain under centering is .67 (PRISM) and .59 (WildChat)
   for BOTH encoders, against .72/.97 and .61/.83 under raw cosine.
3. The shuffle null rejects the pure variance-reduction reading: the last two beat a
   random prior pair of the same role mix (WIN2 - RAND2) by +.015 to +.024 under
   centering in both collections and encoders, so recency carries information; in E5
   raw cosine the same contrast is +.001, which again shows that raw E5 cosine is
   dominated by the common direction. MEAN - RAND2 is about +.04 centered.
   Reading of 1 to 3 together: random pair < last two < last two plus one more user
   turn, with nothing measurable beyond that.
